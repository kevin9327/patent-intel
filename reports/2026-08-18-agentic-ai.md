OpenAI and Anthropic together hold ~100 publications — 0.3% of NVIDIA's
portfolio. The moat-building happens in silicon and infrastructure, while the
labs mostly rely on speed and secrecy instead of patents. DeepMind is the
exception among labs, reflecting Google's institutional filing culture.
